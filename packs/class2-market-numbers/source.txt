Class 2 Mathematics - number sense up to 20 (lesson outline written by the team from the NCERT Class 2 learning outcomes for numbers; replace with the textbook page the teacher is using this week)

Learning goals for this week
- Count objects up to 20 and say how many there are.
- Say the number that comes just before and just after a number up to 20 (one less, one more).
- Count back from 20 to 1.
- Compare two numbers up to 20 and say which is more and which is less.
- Add and take away small numbers (up to 10) using objects or fingers.

Context the class has been using
Children went with their families to the weekly village market (haat). Meena's mother sells tomatoes and onions. Raju's grandfather sells bananas. Children counted things they saw: bananas in a bunch, tomatoes in a basket, coins, goats tied near the well.

Examples the teacher used in class
- A bunch has 12 bananas. One banana is eaten. How many are left? (11)
- Meena puts 5 tomatoes in the basket and then 3 more. How many tomatoes now? (8)
- Which is more: 14 onions or 9 onions? (14)
- What number comes just before 10? (9) Just after 15? (16)
- Counting back from 20 like a rocket launch: 20, 19, 18 ... 1.

Words used in class
more, less, one more, one less, before, after, count back, how many, left, altogether
